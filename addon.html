The addon

Our very own guild addon for WoW: Forever. It swaps out the default guild window for ours, plus a bunch of handy guild stuff. Bless you.
← Guild stats

Hit your guild key (J) and ours pops up instead of Blizzard's. It works by quietly swapping little bits of info with guildies who have it too, so the more of us run it, the better it gets. Here's what it does.

Crafting

Who can make this?

If a guildie can make an item, its tooltip says Guild craftable. The Crafting tab tells you who, and whether they're on right now.

  • Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Enchanting, Engineering, Leatherworking and Tailoring.
  • Scan your recipes once from Home and it keeps up by itself.

Wishlists

Alt-click it, wish for it

Alt-click any item (bags, chat, loot) to stick it on your wishlist. Crafters can see who wants what.

  • Once you get it, it ticks itself off and the guild news cheers you on.
  • Changed your mind? Alt-click it again.

Quests

Quest buddies

Stuck on an elite or dungeon quest? Your quest log shows how many guildies have it too, and who. The Quests tab lets you whisper or invite them in one click.

  • "By place" shows which dungeons people have quests for right now.
  • Only group quests are shared, and you can turn it off.

Members

A roster that gets alts

Everyone's level, class, kills, rank and when they were last on. Set your main once and your alts tuck in underneath it, so one person is one line, not five.

  • Search by name, or see who's online or been away a while.
  • Hover for their note, right-click to whisper or invite.

Something wrong?

Send us a report

  1. Type /iwt report, or hit Diagnostics report in Settings (the cog up top).
  2. A box pops up with it already selected. Ctrl+C to copy.
  3. Paste it on the guild stats page and hit Check report.
  4. You'll see exactly what's in it. Then either Send as a GitHub issue (needs a GitHub account) or Copy for Discord and drop it in the guild Discord.

It's one line starting IWT1.. Nothing secret about it: it's just packed up so it copies cleanly. Inside: versions, recent errors, how fast things ran, sync health and your settings. No chat, no logs, no one else's names. Your own name only goes in if you type /iwt report name.
