The addon

Issues with Tissues is our own guild addon for WoW: Forever. It replaces the default guild panel and adds the things a guild actually needs. Bless you.
← Guild stats

Press your guild key (J by default) and the addon opens instead of Blizzard's guild and communities window. Everything below works by the addon quietly sharing small bits of data with guildmates who also have it, so the more of us run it, the better it gets.

Crafting

Know who can make it

Every item tooltip says Guild craftable when someone in the guild can make it. The Crafting tab finds who, by item or by profession, with their skill level and whether they're online.

  • Crafting professions only: Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Enchanting, Engineering, Leatherworking and Tailoring.
  • Scan your recipes once from Home, and it keeps itself up to date.

Wishlists

Alt-click it, wish for it

Alt-click any item, in your bags, a chat link or loot, to put it on your wishlist. Crafters see who wants what on the Wishlists page and on item tooltips.

  • When the item lands in your bags it ticks itself off, and the guild news says so.
  • Up to 30 items each. Alt-click again to take one off.

Quests

Quest buddies

On an elite or dungeon quest? A small green count in your quest log shows how many guildmates have it too, and its tooltip says who. The Quests tab lists them, online first, with one click to whisper or invite.

  • "By place" shows which dungeons the guild has quests for right now.
  • Only group quests are shared. You can turn it off in Settings.

Members

A roster that knows your alts

The whole guild with level, class, honourable kills, rank and when they were last on. Set your main once and your alts group under it, so a player is one line, not five.

  • Search by name, filter by online or away 7, 14 or 30 days.
  • Hover for their guild note. Right-click to whisper or invite.

Get it

Download Issues with Tissues from CurseForge (or the CurseForge app), then log in and press J. A short welcome card offers to bind your guild key to the addon. It's made for our guild, so it works best when you're in it.

Something wrong? Type /iwt report in game and paste it on the guild stats page.
